How to Check Tournament Rankings

March 19, 2026 15:33 Updated

From your Player Profile, you can view your rankings. To access Tournament Rankings, please follow the steps below.

How to Check your Tournament Rankings

Ranking Unavailable?

 

How to Check your Tournament Rankings

  • Visit USTA.com and click SIGN IN.

 

  • Sign in to your USTA Account. 
    • Choose one of the sign-in options, input your credentials, and sign in.
    • If you need help with signing in, please refer to the USTA Account Troubleshooting Tips.
  • Access the Profile tab.
    • Once you are logged in, click on the User Icon on the top right-hand corner.
    • Click on Profile from the drop-down menu. This will bring you to the Profile Tab.

 

  • From the Player Profile page, click Rankings.

 

  • Review the Rankings tab.
    • On the Rankings tab, you will see your Standing List Rankings for the divisions in which you've earned ranking points from events held in the past 52 weeks.
      • Each Standing List Ranking will display your ranking points earned and your National, Section and District ranking in that division.
      • The publication date is listed for each Standing List.
    • Additionally, you can filter the shown rankings by selecting one of the rankings from the Rank List dropdown to view that specific ranking list.

 

  •  
    • Click down the arrow to show the codes for Tournament and League Match Statuses

 

Ranking Unavailable?

Please note that new players, and those players who have not played a tournament in the past 52 weeks will see “Your ranking is currently unavailable.” in the Rankings tab. If you believe you should have tournament rankings and do not see any, or if you believe the rankings shown are incomplete or incorrect, please follow the steps for your tournament category below. 

  • Junior Tournaments, please fill out the junior form with the player's information and a description of the issue
  • Adult Tournaments, please fill out the adult form with the player's information and a description of the issue
  • Wheelchair tournaments, please email wheelchairtournaments@usta.com

 

If you would instead like to view tournament, league or JTT match results, please refer to the Player Profile Results Tab article.
