The Player Profile Results and Rankings Tab

March 19, 2026 15:34 Updated

On USTA.com, players can view their results.  A player's match results are displayed here for USTA Tournaments, USTA League, Junior Team Tennis, Unsanctioned Tournaments, and Junior Circuit.  You can view all your results or filter your information based on the Competition Category and Year.

Results will only be displayed to players 13 or older by year who have their own profiles.  Players who have not participated in a specific Competition Category or Year cannot select it from the filter list.  There will be no data in the Results tab if the player has no match results.  If you would instead like to view tournament rankings, please refer to How to Check Tournament Rankings.  If you would like to continue accessing match results, please follow the steps outlined below.

How to Access your Results Tab

  • Visit USTA.com and click SIGN IN.

 

  • Sign in to your USTA Account. 
    • Choose one of the sign-in options, input your credentials, and sign in.
    • If you need help with signing in, please refer to the USTA Account Troubleshooting Tips.
  • Access the Profile tab.
    • Once you are logged in, click on the User Icon on the top right-hand corner.
    • Click on Profile from the drop-down menu. This will bring you to the Profile Tab.

 

 

  • View the Results tab.
    • By default, the Profile will display all match results in the Results tab.

 

  • Filter the Results.
    • Click the down arrow to filter the results by the available Competition Categories, such as USTA Tournaments, USTA League, Junior Team Tennis, Unsanctioned Tournaments, and the Junior circuit.
      • The available Competition Categories are based on the types of events the player has participated in.
      • Filtering by USTA Tournaments and rank list allows you to see the points earned at each event for that list.

  • Click down the arrow to show the codes for Tournament and League Match Statuses

 

How to Access the Results Tab on a Linked Child's Profile

  • Visit USTA.com and click SIGN IN.

 

  • Sign in to your USTA Account. 
    • Choose one of the sign-in options, input your credentials, and sign in.
    • If you need help with signing in, please refer to the USTA Account Troubleshooting Tips.
  • Access the Family tab.
    • Once you are logged in, click on the User Icon on the top right-hand corner.
    • When on the Parent role, click on Family from the drop-down menu. This will bring you to the Family tab.
  •  Access the Child's Profile. 
    • Click view profile on the desired child account.

 

  • View the Child's Results tab.
    • By default, the Profile will display all match results in the Results tab.



 

  • Filter the Results.
    • Click the down arrow to filter the results by the available Competition Categories, such as USTA Tournaments, Junior Team Tennis, Unsanctioned Tournaments, and the Junior circuit.
      • The available Competition Categories are based on the types of events the player has participated in.
      • Filtering by USTA Tournaments and a Rank List allows you to see the points earned at each event for that list.

 

  • Tournament Match Statuses
    • Click down the arrow to show the codes for Tournament and League Match Statuses

 

 

How To Search for a Player and Access their Results Tab

  • Visit USTA.com and hover over PLAY.
    • From the drop-down menu, click Search Players.

    

  • Search for the Player.
    • You can search by entering in the player's full name or USTA Number, and pressing the enter key to complete the search.
      • If the search criteria matches a player's profile, you will be brought to their profile page. 
      • If the system doesn't recognize the USTA Number or Full name of the player entered you will receive the error message "Sorry, no exact matches found. Please search using full first name and last name or a USTA ID." Please double check your search criteria and try searching again.

 

  • View the Results tab.
    • By default, the Profile will display all match results in the Results tab.



 

  • Filter the Results.
    • Click the down arrow to filter the results by the available Competition Categories, such as USTA Tournaments, USTA League, Junior Team Tennis, Unsanctioned Tournaments, and the Junior circuit.
      • The available Competition Categories are based on the types of events the player has participated in.
      • Filtering by USTA Tournaments and a Rank List allows you to see the points earned at each event for that list.

 

  • Click down the arrow to show the codes for Tournament and League Match Statuses
