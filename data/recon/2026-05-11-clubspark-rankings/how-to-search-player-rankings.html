How to Search for a Player and View their Tournament Rankings

March 19, 2026 15:34 Updated

You can search for a player and view their tournament rankings directly on USTA.com! To learn how to view these, follow the steps below.

 

Searching for a Player and Viewing Tournament Rankings

  • Visit USTA.com and hover over PLAY.
    • From the drop-down menu, click Search Players.
  • Search for the Player.
    • You can search by entering in the player's full name or USTA Number, and pressing the enter key to complete the search.
      • If the search criteria matches a player's profile, you will be brought to their profile page. 
      • If the system doesn't recognize the USTA Number or Full name of the player entered you will receive the error message "Sorry, no exact matches found. Please search using full first name and last name or a USTA ID." Please double check your search criteria and try searching again.
  • From the Player's Profile, click Rankings.

 

  • Review the Rankings tab.
    • On the Rankings tab, you will see the player's Standing List Rankings for the divisions in which they've earned ranking points from events held in the past 52 weeks.
      • Each Standing List Ranking will display their ranking points earned and their National, Section and District ranking in that division.
      • The publication date is listed for each Standing List.
    • Additionally, you can filter the shown rankings by selecting one of the rankings from the Rank List dropdown to view that specific ranking list.
